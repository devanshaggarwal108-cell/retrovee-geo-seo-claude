# Retail Ai Visibility

Test whether visible page text answers what the product does, what is included, what it does not do, and where it ships. Draft short answers grounded in verified product details. Keep dimensions, run time and compatibility marked unknown until confirmed for the exact SKU.
Distinguish crawler access, index eligibility, citations and recommendations. For AI answer spot checks, record the exact prompt, locale, date and sources. A single run cannot establish a trend. Treat special AI files or markup as optional experiments with a stated hypothesis, not requirements for Google AI features.
Example: explain that a vinyl-style Bluetooth speaker streams audio from a paired device and list its verified box contents in visible text.

Save findings and proposed edits in a local draft. Include current and proposed values, evidence, and the checks to run after release. Live changes and publishing require the user’s approval after reviewing the draft.
